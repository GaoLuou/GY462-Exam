## Q1a — Main opportunities of investing in real estate, and how to 'maximise' these? (10 pts)

*(Lecture 1)*

### Standard Answer

Direct real estate investment offers three main opportunities:

**1. Terminal value (capital appreciation)**
The exit price at the end of the holding period is typically the dominant source of return. To maximise it: buy below intrinsic value (through rigorous DCF analysis), actively manage the asset to grow NOI, and time the exit when cap rates are compressing — since Value = NOI / Cap Rate, even a small fall in the cap rate at exit significantly increases the sale price.

**2. Rental income growth**
Long-term leases provide stable cash flows, and rent reviews allow rents to be reset to market levels. To maximise: negotiate leases with upward-only rent reviews, target markets with strong rental demand fundamentals.

**3. Operating cost savings**
Reducing operating expenditure directly increases NOI. To maximise: use FRI (full repairing and insuring) leases to transfer cost risk to tenants, and invest in energy efficiency improvements.

---

### 中文解释

**三大机会，考试只需记住这个框架：**

| 机会 | 如何最大化 |
|------|---------|
| 终值/资本增值（Terminal Value） | 低买高卖 + 主动资产管理 + 在 Cap Rate 压缩时退出 |
| 租金增长（Rental Growth） | 租约中写入向上调整条款 + 选强需求市场 |
| 运营成本节约（OpEx Savings） | FRI 租约转嫁成本 + 能效改造 |

**关键公式（必须记住）：** Value = NOI / Cap Rate。 Cap Rate 下降 → 分母变小 → 终值上升。这就是为什么"时机选择"极其重要。

**关键术语：** Terminal Value 终值 / Cap Rate 资本化率 / NOI（Net Operating Income）净营业收入 / FRI Lease 全修全保租约 / Rent Review 租金审查

## Q1b — How does your answer change for a 3–5 year vs 10–15 year horizon? (10 pts)

*(Lecture 1 & 2)*

### Standard Answer

**Short-term horizon (3–5 years): terminal value dominates**
With a short hold, there is limited time to collect rental income, so the strategy depends almost entirely on capital appreciation — buying at the right price and selling at a profit. The investor must form a view on near-term market cycles (cap rate movements, credit conditions). Lease structure matters less because the investor will likely exit within a single lease.

**Long-term horizon (10–15 years): income and rental growth compound**
Over a longer hold, the cumulative effect of rental income and rental growth becomes the primary driver of return. The investor benefits from multiple rent review cycles and lease renewals (inter-lease reversion). Lease covenant quality (tenant creditworthiness), lease length, and rent review frequency become the critical variables. The investor is also more exposed to inter-lease risk — the uncertainty of re-letting at market rents when a lease expires.

The key distinction: short-term investors buy the market cycle; long-term investors buy the income stream.

---

### 中文解释

**核心逻辑：持有时间决定哪种机会最重要。**

- **短期（3–5年）**：租金收入有限，回报几乎完全来自资本利得（Capital Appreciation）。必须精准判断市场周期——买错价格或卖错时机都会损失巨大。
- **长期（10–15年）**：复利效应使租金增长成为主导。跨越多个租约周期，每次租金复归（Rental Reversion）都是机会。但同时，租约到期重新招租（Inter-Lease Risk）的不确定性也增大。

**关键术语：** Rental Reversion 租金复归 / Inter-Lease Risk 租约间风险（重新招租的不确定性） / Lease Covenant 租户信用质量 / Capital Appreciation 资本增值

## Q1c — Main risks of real estate investment and how to mitigate them? (10 pts)

*(Lecture 1)*

### Standard Answer

Real estate has five key downside risks:

**1. Vacancy risk** — the property is unlet, generating no income while costs continue.
*Mitigation:* thorough tenant due diligence; multi-tenant assets to spread occupancy risk; maintain CapEx reserves for re-letting.

**2. Tenant default risk** — an existing tenant fails to pay rent or goes bankrupt.
*Mitigation:* credit-check tenants at acquisition; require parent guarantees or rent deposits.

**3. Liquidity risk** — the asset cannot be sold quickly or at a fair price. Transactions take months and transaction costs are high (5–10% in the UK).
*Mitigation:* invest in well-located, institutionally liquid assets; hold adequate cash reserves to avoid forced sales.

**4. Operating cost risk** — maintenance, insurance, and compliance costs exceed budget, compressing NOI.
*Mitigation:* use FRI leases; conduct thorough technical due diligence before purchase.

**5. Leverage risk** — debt amplifies all of the above risks. A fall in NOI or asset value is magnified at the equity level.
*Mitigation:* maintain conservative LTV; use fixed-rate debt; monitor DCR regularly.

---

### 中文解释

**五大风险框架（按重要性排列）：**

| 风险 | 一句话缓解方法 |
|------|-------------|
| 空置（Vacancy） | 多租户 + 预留 CapEx |
| 租户违约（Tenant Default） | 信用审查 + 担保 |
| 流动性差（Liquidity） | 优质区位 + 现金储备 |
| 运营成本超支（OpEx） | FRI 租约 + 技术尽调 |
| 杠杆（Leverage） | 保守 LTV + 固息债务 |

**重点：** 杠杆风险是所有风险的放大器（Amplifier）——它不制造新风险，但让其他四类风险的冲击成倍传导到股权层面。

**关键术语：** CapEx（Capital Expenditure）资本支出储备 / LTV（Loan-to-Value）贷款价值比 / DCR（Debt Coverage Ratio）偿债覆盖率 / FRI Lease 全修全保租约

## Q1d — How do 'green investment' trends change the perspective on risks and opportunities? (15 pts)

*(Lecture 10: S Burak Kaplanoglu, "ESG and Real Estate")*

### Standard Answer

Green investment trends — captured under the ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) framework — have created a **two-tier market** in real estate, fundamentally changing both the risk and opportunity profile.

**New risks:**

**1. Stranded asset risk**
Buildings that fail to meet evolving energy efficiency standards face longer void periods, difficulty obtaining insurance, and valuation write-downs. The lecture estimates climate change could wipe 3.4–10% of housing value globally ($25 trillion). Investors holding non-compliant assets may find them unlettable or unsaleable — a "stranded asset."

**2. Regulatory and financing risk**
73% of lenders already have an ESG lending strategy, and 81% say a firm's ESG status will increasingly affect their appetite to lend. Non-compliant assets face higher borrowing costs and reduced access to institutional capital. Additionally, the Equator Principles require borrowers from 128 signatory banks to meet environmental and social standards on large-scale projects.

**New opportunities:**

**1. Green premium**
Green-certified buildings command measurably higher rents and lower cap rates (higher valuations). Evidence: London offices carry an 11.6% green premium, North America 7.1%, Asia 9.9% (JLL, cited in lecture). An investor who upgrades assets ahead of market demand captures this premium.

**2. Cheaper financing**
ESG-compliant assets access green loans and green bonds at lower rates, and attract a broader institutional investor base.

**Overall:** the lecture's key message is: *"less focus on the label, more focus on the risks and opportunities"* — ESG is not just ethics, it directly affects asset valuations, void periods, financing costs, and exit realisations.

---

### 中文解释

**核心逻辑：ESG 把"非财务表现"与"财务表现"直接挂钩。**

讲座核心命题：Business Performance = Financial + Non-Financial Performance。绿色投资趋势正在制造一个**双轨市场（Two-Tier Market）**：
- 绿色资产：低 Cap Rate（高估值）+ 低融资成本 + 更广投资者群体
- 棕色资产：高 Cap Rate（低估值）+ 融资受限 + 搁浅风险

**新风险两条最重要：**
1. 搁浅资产（Stranded Asset）：建筑不达标 → 空置期延长 → 无法投保 → 价值归零
2. 融资成本上升：73% 贷款机构有 ESG 策略，不合规资产贷款更贵/更难

**新机会必须引用数据：**
- 伦敦绿色溢价 **11.6%**，北美 **7.1%**，亚洲 **9.9%**（来源 Lec10）

**关键术语：** ESG（Environmental, Social, Governance）/ Stranded Asset 搁浅资产 / Green Premium 绿色溢价 / Brown Discount 棕色折价 / Equator Principles 赤道原则 / Two-Tier Market 双轨市场

## Q1e — Indivisibility: meaning and effect on risks and opportunities? (10 pts)

*(Lecture 1 & 2)*

### Standard Answer

**Definition:** Indivisibility means that direct real estate assets cannot be broken into smaller units. Unlike shares, a property must be acquired as a whole — creating a large minimum lot size.

**Effects on risks:**

- **Forced leverage:** Because individual properties cost millions, investors must use debt. Leverage amplifies all underlying risks — a fall in NOI or asset value is multiplied at the equity level.
- **Concentration risk:** The high cost per asset means investors hold few assets, unable to diversify away idiosyncratic (property-specific) risk. A single tenant default or local market downturn can devastate the portfolio.
- **Liquidity risk:** Large, bespoke assets have a small pool of buyers; transactions take months and cannot be partially liquidated.

**Effect on opportunities:**

- **Leveraged upside:** When property yield exceeds cost of debt (positive leverage), equity returns significantly exceed unlevered returns.
- **Active management alpha:** Inability to diversify forces investors to manage each asset intensively, creating opportunities to add value through refurbishment, re-leasing, and repositioning.

---

### 中文解释

**不可分割性是 Q1e/f/g 三题的核心概念，必须掌握。**

- **定义**：直接房地产无法拆分 → 最低投资门槛极高（Large Lot Size）
- **三大风险链条**：大金额 → 必须借贷（Forced Leverage）→ 资产数量少（Concentration Risk）→ 难以变现（Liquidity Risk）
- **机会**：同一杠杆，在上行市场中放大收益（Positive Leverage = Property Yield > Cost of Debt）

**关键术语：** Indivisibility 不可分割性 / Large Lot Size 大宗资产规模 / Concentration Risk 集中度风险 / Positive Leverage 正杠杆 / Idiosyncratic Risk 非系统性/特殊风险

## Q1f — How does indivisibility affect portfolio construction? What can an asset manager do? (15 pts)

*(Dr. Szumilo's Portfolio Structuring Lecture)*

### Standard Answer

**The problem:**
Modern portfolio theory shows that diversification reduces unsystematic risk. However, indivisibility makes diversification extremely difficult in direct real estate:

- Each asset requires a large capital commitment, so meaningful diversification across sectors and geographies requires hundreds of millions of pounds — inaccessible to most investors.
- With only 5–10 assets, the portfolio remains highly concentrated; the variance-reduction benefits of diversification are limited.
- High transaction costs (5–10% in the UK) and slow deal timelines prevent dynamic rebalancing when market conditions change.

**What asset managers can do:**

1. **Pool capital through funds** — aggregate multiple investors' capital into a single vehicle, enabling the fund to buy more assets and achieve better diversification than any individual investor could.
2. **Use indirect vehicles (REITs, unlisted funds)** — these bypass indivisibility entirely by giving investors fractional exposure to a portfolio (see Q1g).
3. **Selective leverage** — borrowing allows the same equity base to be spread across more assets, increasing the asset count and reducing concentration risk (though introducing financial risk).

---

### 中文解释

**核心矛盾：MPT（现代投资组合理论）要求多元化，但不可分割性使多元化极其昂贵。**

**三大障碍：**
1. 单项资产价值高 → 有效分散所需资本极高
2. 资产数量少（5–10 项）→ 分散化效果有限，特殊风险（Idiosyncratic Risk）无法消除
3. 交易成本高 + 交易周期长 → 无法像股票那样动态再平衡（Rebalancing）

**资产管理人的三条出路：**
- 基金结构（集合资金）→ 买更多资产
- 间接投资工具（REITs/非上市基金）→ 绕过不可分割性
- 合理运用杠杆 → 同等资本覆盖更多资产

**关键术语：** MPT（Modern Portfolio Theory）现代投资组合理论 / Diversification 分散化 / Rebalancing 动态再平衡 / Transaction Costs 交易成本

## Q1g — 'Indirect investment allows to by-pass indivisibility' — what does it mean? (5 pts)

*(Lecture 2)*

### Standard Answer

When investing indirectly — for example by buying REIT shares — the investor purchases a financial security that represents a fractional claim on a portfolio of physical properties. The REIT owns the indivisible assets; the investor buys shares in the REIT, which are fully divisible and trade on a stock exchange.

This means:
- Any capital amount can be invested (no minimum lot size constraint).
- A single share provides exposure to a diversified portfolio of many properties.
- Shares can be sold instantly on the market, eliminating the illiquidity of direct investment.

In short, indirect investment converts an indivisible, illiquid real asset into a divisible, liquid financial security — effectively bypassing the core constraint of direct investment.

---

### 中文解释

**一句话理解：** REIT 把"无法拆分的楼"变成了"可以买一股的股票"。

- REIT 持有实体资产（不可分割）→ 将所有权分割为股票（完全可分割）→ 投资者买股票，间接获得物业敞口
- 结果：可买任意金额 + 自动多元化 + 随时买卖

**关键术语：** Indirect Investment 间接投资 / Divisibility 可分割性 / Securitisation of Ownership 所有权证券化 / REIT（Real Estate Investment Trust）房地产投资信托基金

## Q1h — REITs: benefits and downsides for investors buying REIT shares? (15 pts)

*(REITs Lecture: Prof. Silva and Mr. Salway)*

### Standard Answer

**Benefits:**

- **Liquidity:** REIT shares trade on public stock exchanges — investors can buy or sell in seconds, compared to months for direct real estate.
- **Divisibility and accessibility:** Any capital amount can be invested; retail investors with limited capital gain exposure to commercial real estate.
- **Diversification:** A single REIT holds many properties across sectors and geographies, instantly solving the concentration problem of direct investment.
- **Tax efficiency:** REITs are structured to pass income through to investors without corporate-level tax — avoiding double taxation. They are required to distribute 90%+ of taxable income as dividends.

**Downsides:**

- **Loss of diversification benefit:** The key theoretical reason to hold real estate is its low correlation with equities. However, listed REIT shares behave like stocks — correlations with equity markets spike precisely during crises, when diversification is most needed. The indirect "wrapper" partially destroys the portfolio diversification benefit.
- **NAV discount/premium:** REIT share prices are driven by market sentiment and can trade significantly above (premium) or below (discount) the net asset value of the underlying properties — introducing a pricing risk that does not exist in direct investment.
- **No control:** Investors are passive minority shareholders with no say over asset selection or management decisions.

---

### 中文解释

**考试最重要的点：最大的缺点是"丧失多元化收益"（Loss of Diversification Benefit）。**

投资者选择房地产的核心动机之一是它与股市的低相关性（Low Correlation with Equities）。但上市 REIT 本质上是股票，危机时与大盘高度相关——恰好在最需要对冲的时候失效。

**优缺点对比表：**

| 优势 | 缺点 |
|------|------|
| 流动性（Liquidity）：实时交易 | 与股市相关性高（Equity Correlation）|
| 可分割性：任意金额投资 | NAV 折/溢价：价格偏离真实资产价值 |
| 多元化：一股 = 多项物业敞口 | 无控制权（No Control）|
| 税务透传：避免双重征税 | |
| 强制分配（≥90%）：稳定股息 | |

**关键术语：** NAV（Net Asset Value）资产净值 / NAV Discount/Premium NAV 折价/溢价 / Dividend Pass-Through 股息透传 / Equity Market Correlation 股市相关性

## Q1i — Main benefits and costs for asset managers operating as a REIT? (10 pts)

*(REITs Lecture: Mr. Salway)*

### Standard Answer

**Benefits for asset managers:**

- **Permanent capital:** Unlike a closed-end fund (which has a fixed life and must return capital), a REIT has no maturity date — it can continuously raise capital by issuing new shares, supporting long-term investment strategies.
- **Scale and market access:** As a listed entity, a REIT can grow its asset base over time and access institutional debt markets (public bonds, credit facilities) on favourable terms unavailable to smaller private vehicles.
- **Tax advantage as a fundraising tool:** The REIT's pass-through tax structure attracts investors, making it easier to raise capital.

**Costs for asset managers:**

- **Mandatory payout constrains reinvestment:** The legal requirement to distribute 90%+ of income means the manager cannot retain earnings for reinvestment. Every acquisition or upgrade requires returning to capital markets — creating dependency on market timing.
- **Regulatory burden:** REITs must comply with ongoing disclosure requirements (quarterly reporting, REIT qualification tests), creating compliance costs and limiting strategic flexibility.
- **Short-term shareholder pressure:** Listed shareholders may push for short-term dividend maximisation, conflicting with the long-duration nature of real estate investing.

**Core tension:** the REIT's greatest advantage (permanent capital) and greatest constraint (mandatory distribution) are two sides of the same coin — capital never needs to be returned, but it can never be retained either.

---

### 中文解释

**本题从资产管理人（而非投资者）视角作答。**

**最重要的对立关系：**
- **优势**：永久资本（Permanent Capital）——无需还本，持续运营
- **代价**：强制分配（Mandatory Distribution ≥90%）——每次再投资都要重新去市场融资

这个矛盾就是 REIT 结构的核心张力：资本不用还，但也不能留。

| 优势 | 成本 |
|------|------|
| 永久资本（无到期日） | 强制分配 → 无法留存用于再投资 |
| 规模经济 + 机构融资渠道 | 合规成本高（季报、资格测试）|
| 税务透传 → 吸引投资者 | 短期股东压力（分红 vs 长期价值）|

**关键术语：** Permanent Capital 永久资本 / Mandatory Distribution 强制分配 / REIT Qualification Tests REIT 资格测试 / Activist Investor 激进投资者

# QUESTION 2 — Mortgage Analysis

**Background:** £1,500,000 property. Income £5,000/month. Savings £750,000. 30-year mortgage. DCR = 1.3. Interest rate 4.5% p.a., monthly compounding (i = 0.375%/month). Origination charge = 1%.

---

## Q2a — CPM with £500k deposit: monthly payment and DCR qualification? (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

Loan = £1,500,000 − £500,000 = **£1,000,000**

$$PMT = 1{,}000{,}000 \times \frac{0.00375}{1-(1.00375)^{-360}} = \mathbf{£5{,}066.85}$$

DCR check: maximum allowable payment = £5,000 / 1.3 = £3,846. Since £5,066.85 > £3,846, **the borrower does NOT qualify.**

---

### 中文解释

**CPM 公式：** $PMT = L \times \frac{i}{1-(1+i)^{-n}}$

**DCR 检验：** 月供不得超过 月收入 ÷ DCR = £5,000 ÷ 1.3 = £3,846。£5,066 > £3,846 → 不合格。

**关键术语：** CPM（Constant Payment Mortgage）等额还款抵押贷款 / DCR（Debt Coverage Ratio）偿债覆盖率

---

## Q2b — Use all savings as deposit: LTV, monthly payment, DCR qualification? (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

Loan = £1,500,000 − £750,000 = **£750,000**

LTV = 750,000 / 1,500,000 = **50%**

$$PMT = 750{,}000 \times \frac{0.00375}{1-(1.00375)^{-360}} = \mathbf{£3{,}800.14}$$

Required income = £3,800.14 × 1.3 = £4,940 < £5,000 ✓ **The borrower qualifies (just barely).**

*(Note: the official exam PDF states £3,900.14 — this is a typo. The correct answer is £3,800.14, confirmed by Q2i.)*

---

### 中文解释

LTV = 贷款额 / 房产价值 = 50%。月供 £3,800.14，所需月收入 £4,940 < 实际 £5,000 → 勉强通过。

**注意：** 官方 PDF 的 £3,900.14 是笔误，正确值 £3,800.14（线性关系验证：£5,066.85 × 0.75 = £3,800）。

**关键术语：** LTV（Loan-to-Value）贷款价值比 / Marginal Qualification 边缘通过

---

## Q2c — Advantages and disadvantages of loan (b) vs (a)? (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

**Advantage:** The borrower qualifies — loan (b) is the only viable option under the DCR constraint.

**Disadvantages:**
- All savings are committed as a deposit, leaving zero liquidity buffer for unexpected expenses.
- The borrower is self-employed, so £5,000/month is an estimate, not a guaranteed salary. Any income shortfall — even modest — would push DCR below 1.3, risking bank alarm or default.
- The qualification margin is razor-thin (DCR = 1.316), providing almost no safety buffer.

---

### 中文解释

**优点只有一个：** 方案 b 是唯一能通过 DCR 的方案。

**缺点核心逻辑：** 方案 b 的通过本身制造了脆弱性——零储备 + 自雇收入不稳定 + 边缘合格，任何小扰动都可能导致违约（Default）。

**关键术语：** Liquidity Buffer 流动性缓冲 / Default 违约 / Self-Employed Income 自雇收入

---

## Q2d — Effective cost of borrowing at maturity, 15 years, and 5 years? (15 pts)

### Standard Answer

**Net proceeds** = £750,000 × 99% = **£742,500** (1% origination charge deducted upfront).

ECB = IRR of cash flows: receive £742,500 at t=0; pay PMT each month; pay outstanding balance at repayment date.

**At maturity (360 months):** 360 × £3,800.14 payments, no balloon.
→ **ECB = 4.5859%**

**Repay after 15 years:** Outstanding balance B₁₈₀ = **£496,754.67**
→ CF: 180 payments + £496,754.67 at month 180 → **ECB = 4.6%**

**Repay after 5 years:** Outstanding balance B₆₀ = **£683,684.38**
→ CF: 60 payments + £683,684.38 at month 60 → **ECB = 4.7344%**

| Repayment | Outstanding Balance | ECB |
|-----------|-------------------|-----|
| 30 years (maturity) | — | 4.5859% |
| 15 years | £496,754.67 | 4.60% |
| 5 years | £683,684.38 | 4.7344% |

---

### 中文解释

**ECB = 借款人实际现金流的 IRR。** 因为起始费用使实际到手金额从 £750k 减为 £742,500，月供却不变，所以 ECB > 名义利率（4.5%）。

**计算工具：** TI BA II+ 的 CF Worksheet，输入 t=0 流出 £742,500 + 各期月供 + 期末剩余本金，求 IRR × 12。

**关键术语：** ECB（Effective Cost of Borrowing）有效借款成本 / Origination Charge 起始费用 / Outstanding Balance 未偿还贷款余额 / IRR 内部收益率

---

## Q2e — What happens to ECB when you repay sooner? (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

ECB **increases** the sooner the loan is repaid (4.5859% < 4.6% < 4.7344%).

The origination charge (£7,500) is a one-time upfront sunk cost. When held to maturity, it is amortised over 360 months — a small annual burden. When repaid after 5 years, the same cost is amortised over only 60 months — a much larger proportional cost. The earlier the repayment, the more concentrated this fixed cost, and the higher the annualised ECB.

---

### 中文解释

**一句话总结：** 起始费用是沉没成本（Sunk Cost），提前还款 = 在更短时间内摊销 → ECB 越高。

**关键术语：** Sunk Cost 沉没成本 / Amortisation 摊销 / Annualised ECB 年化有效借款成本

## Q2f — Interest rate with 2% inflation (neglect monthly compounding)? (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

The correct approach uses the Fisher equation — NOT simple addition:

$$(1 + r_{nominal}) = (1 + r_{real}) \times (1 + \pi) = 1.045 \times 1.02 = 1.0659$$

$$r_{nominal} = \mathbf{6.59\%}$$

Simple addition (4.5% + 2% = 6.5%) is incorrect — it ignores the cross-product term (0.045 × 0.02 = 0.09%), giving an approximation, not the exact answer.

---

### 中文解释

**费雪方程（Fisher Equation）：** $(1+r_{名义}) = (1+r_{实际})(1+\pi)$，乘法不是加法。

考试陷阱：4.5% + 2% = 6.5% **是错的**，正确答案是 **6.59%**。

**关键术语：** Fisher Equation 费雪方程 / Nominal Rate 名义利率 / Real Rate 实际利率 / Cross-Product Term 交叉项（被加法近似忽略的 0.09%）

## Q2g — Discuss the 'inflation tilt' problem and why it is non-linear. (5 pts)

*(Lecture 7)*

### Standard Answer

When a nominal interest rate embeds inflation (e.g., 6.59%), the nominal monthly payment is constant — but its **real value** (purchasing power) declines over time:

$$\text{Real payment}_t = \frac{PMT_{nominal}}{(1+\pi)^t}$$

This means the real debt burden is **highest in the early years** and falls over time. The problem: borrowers are typically at their lowest income in the early career years — exactly when the real payment burden is greatest. This mismatch is the inflation tilt.

**Why non-linear:** $(1+\pi)^t$ grows exponentially, so the decline in real burden accelerates over time — it is front-loaded and convex, not a straight line.

**Problems for borrowers:** (1) Reduced affordability in early years, (2) Difficulty qualifying under DCR even when the loan is sustainable long-term (as shown in Q2h).

---

### 中文解释

**核心概念：** 名义月供不变，但通胀让每期月供的实际购买力逐年下降 → 早期实际负担重，后期轻 → 产生"前置倾斜"（Front-Loading）。

**非线性原因：** 实际月供按 $(1+\pi)^t$ 指数衰减，而非线性递减。

**对借款人的两个问题：**
1. 职业早期（收入最低时）实际还款压力最大——逆生命周期
2. 名义月供升高 → DCR 不达标（Q2h 中体现）

**关键术语：** Inflation Tilt Problem 通胀倾斜问题 / Front-Loading 前置负担 / Real vs Nominal Payment 实际 vs 名义月供 / Non-Linear 非线性

## Q2h — Monthly payment at 6.59% and ECB at maturity? (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

Monthly rate = 6.59% / 12 = 0.5492%

$$PMT = 750{,}000 \times \frac{0.005492}{1-(1.005492)^{-360}} = \mathbf{£4{,}784.99}$$

**DCR check:** Required income = £4,784.99 × 1.3 = £6,220 > £5,000 → **Does NOT qualify.**

**ECB at maturity:** Net proceeds £742,500; 360 payments of £4,784.99.
→ **ECB = 6.6874%**

**What to notice:** The borrower who barely qualified at 4.5% (Q2b) is entirely priced out at 6.59%. This is the inflation tilt problem in practice — the higher nominal rate needed to preserve the bank's real return makes the loan unaffordable, even though the borrower's long-run repayment capacity is unchanged.

---

### 中文解释

**关键对比（必须记住）：**

| 利率 | 月供 | 所需收入（×1.3）| 实际收入 | 结果 |
|------|-----|-------------|-------|------|
| 4.5% | £3,800 | £4,940 | £5,000 | 通过 |
| 6.59% | £4,785 | £6,220 | £5,000 | **不通过** |

ECB = 6.6874%（超出名义利率约 0.1%，原因同 Q2d：起始费用摊销）。

**关键术语：** Inflation Tilt 通胀倾斜 / DCR Barrier DCR 资格障碍

## Q2i — ARM: three monthly payments in three periods? (15 pts)

*(Lecture 7)*

### Standard Answer

An ARM is a **partitioned CPM**: at each rate reset, recalculate PMT using remaining balance and remaining term.

**Period 1 (Months 1–24, rate = 4.5%, i = 0.375%, n = 360):**
$$PMT_1 = 750{,}000 \times \frac{0.00375}{1-(1.00375)^{-360}} = \mathbf{£3{,}800.14}$$

Balance after 24 months: $B_{24} = \mathbf{£725{,}245.76}$

**Period 2 (Months 25–48, rate = 5.5%, i = 0.4583%, n = 336):**
$$PMT_2 = 725{,}245.76 \times \frac{0.004583}{1-(1.004583)^{-336}} = \mathbf{£4{,}235}$$

Balance after 24 months: $B_{48} = \mathbf{£702{,}186}$

**Period 3 (Months 49–360, rate = 6.5%, i = 0.5417%, n = 312):**
$$PMT_3 = 702{,}186 \times \frac{0.005417}{1-(1.005417)^{-312}} = \mathbf{£4{,}668.96}$$

| Period | Months | Rate | PMT | Opening Balance |
|--------|--------|------|-----|-----------------|
| 1 | 1–24 | 4.5% | £3,800.14 | £750,000 |
| 2 | 25–48 | 5.5% | £4,235.00 | £725,245.76 |
| 3 | 49–360 | 6.5% | £4,668.96 | £702,186.00 |

---

### 中文解释

**ARM 核心逻辑：** 每次利率重置 = 以剩余本金 + 剩余期限重新算一个新的 CPM。

三步走：① 算第一期月供和期末余额 → ② 以该余额和剩余期限算第二期 → ③ 同理算第三期。

**关键术语：** ARM（Adjustable Rate Mortgage）可调利率抵押贷款 / Partitioned CPM 分段等额还款 / Rate Reset 利率重置 / Outstanding Balance 剩余本金

## Q2j — ECB of ARM at maturity and at Year 5? Discuss. (15 pts)

### Standard Answer

**ECB at maturity:**
Net proceeds = £742,500. Cash flows: 24 × £3,800.14 + 24 × £4,235 + 312 × £4,668.96.
→ **ECB = 6.0966%**

This is lower than the fixed-rate loan at 6.59% (ECB = 6.6874%), because the ARM benefits from 4.5% for the first two years, pulling the blended cost below the fixed rate.

**ECB if repaid at end of Year 5 (Month 60):**
At month 60, the ARM has been in Period 3 (6.5%) for 12 months.

$$B_{60} = 702{,}186 \times (1.005417)^{12} - 4{,}668.96 \times \frac{(1.005417)^{12}-1}{0.005417} = \mathbf{£691{,}485.19}$$

CFs: 24 × £3,800.14 + 24 × £4,235 + 11 × £4,668.96 + (£4,668.96 + £691,485.19) at month 60.
→ **ECB = 5.4676%**

**What do you notice?**
This is **counterintuitive** compared to Q2e: early repayment gives a *lower* ECB, not higher. The reason: by repaying at Year 5, the borrower avoids 25 years of the 6.5% rate. The saving from escaping the high-rate period far outweighs the increased amortisation burden of the origination charge. The dominant cost driver here is the high long-term rate, not the upfront fee.

| Scenario | ECB |
|----------|-----|
| Fixed 6.59%, maturity | 6.6874% |
| ARM, maturity | 6.0966% |
| ARM, repay Year 5 | **5.4676%** |

---

### 中文解释

**两个关键对比：**

**1. ARM vs 固定利率（同样持有至到期）：**
ARM（6.0966%）< 固定利率（6.6874%）——因为 ARM 前两年享受 4.5% 低利率，拉低了整体加权成本。

**2. ARM 提前还款（反直觉！）：**
- Q2e 结论：固定利率提前还款 → ECB 升高（起始费用摊销集中）
- Q2j 结论：ARM 提前还款 → ECB **降低**（逃离了未来 25 年的 6.5% 高利率期）

**核心逻辑：** 哪个成本因素占主导？
- 固定利率：唯一的额外成本是起始费用 → 提前还款让它更贵
- ARM：主要成本是长期的高利率 → 提前还款让你逃离高成本 → ECB 下降

**关键术语：** Counterintuitive Result 反直觉结论 / Dominant Cost Driver 主导成本因素 / Blended ECB 混合有效借款成本

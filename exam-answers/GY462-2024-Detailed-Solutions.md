## Q1a — Main risks and opportunities of real estate investment (10 pts)

*(Lecture 1)*

### Standard Answer

**Risks:**

- **Vacancy:** the property may be unlet, generating no income while fixed costs continue.
- **Tenant default:** an existing tenant fails to pay rent or goes bankrupt mid-lease.
- **Operating cost overruns:** maintenance, insurance and management costs exceed budget, compressing NOI.
- **Liquidity risk:** real estate transactions take months and cost 5–10% in fees; forced sales at distressed prices are a real risk.
- **Leverage:** most RE investments use debt, which amplifies all of the above — a fall in NOI or asset value hits equity first and disproportionately.

**Opportunities:**

- **Terminal value:** the exit sale price is typically the dominant source of total return, especially over longer holds.
- **Rental growth:** rent reviews allow income to grow over time, compounding NOI.
- **Operating cost savings:** efficient management and energy efficiency improvements raise NOI.
- **Positive leverage:** when property yield > cost of debt, leverage amplifies equity returns beyond the unlevered property return.

---

### 中文解释

**五大风险 + 四大机会是 Lecture 1 的核心框架。**

关键点：杠杆（Leverage）既是风险也是机会的放大器——当 Property Yield > Cost of Debt（正杠杆 Positive Leverage）时放大收益；反之则加剧损失。Terminal Value 通常是最重要的机会，课件明确提出："Which one is more important?"

**关键术语：** Vacancy 空置 / Tenant Default 租户违约 / Liquidity Risk 流动性风险 / Leverage 杠杆 / Terminal Value 终值 / Rental Growth 租金增长 / Positive Leverage 正杠杆 / NOI（Net Operating Income）净营业收入

## Q1b — What makes real estate different from other financial assets? (5 pts)

*(Lecture 1)*

### Standard Answer

Direct real estate differs from financial assets (stocks, bonds) in four structural ways:

1. **Indivisibility and large lot size:** properties must be bought whole — no fractional ownership. This forces investors to use leverage and creates high minimum capital thresholds.
2. **Heterogeneity:** every property is unique in location, age, and tenant profile. There is no standardised product; pricing relies on professional judgement, creating information asymmetry.
3. **Slow and expensive transactions:** deals take months and cost 5–10% in stamp duty, legal fees and agent fees — versus near-instant, near-zero-cost equity trades.
4. **Private trading and poor liquidity:** most deals are negotiated privately with no centralised exchange. Price discovery is opaque, and the market is thin.

These four features collectively explain why real estate commands a liquidity premium in its discount rate.

---

### 中文解释

**四大结构性差异（Lecture 1 必考点）：**

| 特征 | 核心影响 |
|------|---------|
| 不可分割性（Indivisibility）| 必须整体买入 → 被迫用杠杆 |
| 异质性（Heterogeneity）| 无标准化定价 → 信息不对称 |
| 交易缓慢昂贵（Slow/Expensive Transactions）| 5–10% 交易成本 → 流动性差 |
| 私下交易（Private Trading）| 无集中市场 → 价格发现效率低 |

**关键术语：** Indivisibility 不可分割性 / Heterogeneity 异质性 / Liquidity Premium 流动性溢价 / Price Discovery 价格发现

## Q1c — How does DCF account for risks and opportunities? (5 pts)

*(Lectures 1 & 2)*

### Standard Answer

DCF incorporates risks and opportunities through two mechanisms:

**1. Cash flow forecasting (capturing opportunities):**
The Pro Forma structure forces explicit modelling of every opportunity and risk driver:
- PGI − Vacancy + Other Income − OpEx = **NOI**
- NOI − Capital Improvements = **PBTCF**
- Plus terminal (resale) value at exit, typically estimated as NOI_{n+1} / Going-Out Cap Rate.

**2. Discount rate (pricing risk):**
The discount rate r = r_f + Risk Premium reflects the riskiness of the cash flows. Critically, different cash flows use different rates (Lecture 2):
- **Intra-lease cash flows** (contracted rent): lower rate — primary risk is only tenant default.
- **Inter-lease and terminal value**: higher rate — full market uncertainty about re-letting conditions and exit pricing.

NPV > 0 means the asset is underpriced relative to its risk; IRR vs hurdle rate drives the buy/hold/sell decision.

---

### 中文解释

**DCF 的两个核心机制：**

1. **现金流预测 = 量化机会与风险**：用 Pro Forma 结构（PGI → NOI → PBTCF）强制投资者对每个风险驱动因素明确建模。
2. **折现率 = 风险定价**：不同风险程度的现金流用不同折现率——租约内（Intra-Lease，低折现率）vs 租约间及终值（Inter-Lease，高折现率）。

**关键术语：** PGI（Potential Gross Income）潜在总收入 / NOI（Net Operating Income）净营业收入 / PBTCF（Property Before-Tax Cash Flow）税前物业现金流 / Going-Out Cap Rate 退出资本化率 / Intra-Lease 租约内 / Inter-Lease 租约间 / Hurdle Rate 门槛回报率

## Q1d — Key elements of operating Pro Forma and NOI estimation (15 pts)

*(Lecture 2)*

### Standard Answer

The Pro Forma structure flows as:

**PGI** (Potential Gross Income) = fully-leased rental income
**− Vacancy allowance** = assumed void rate applied to PGI (never assume 100% occupancy)
**+ Other income** = parking, signage, miscellaneous
**= EGI** (Effective Gross Income)
**− Operating expenses** = fixed costs (insurance, property tax, management fees) + variable costs (maintenance). Excludes depreciation and income tax — these are accounting items, not cash flows.
**= NOI** (Net Operating Income) — the key performance metric
**− Capital improvements (CI)** = tenant improvements (fit-out costs to attract/retain tenants) + leasing commissions
**= PBTCF** (Property Before-Tax Cash Flow)

**Key estimation considerations:**
- Vacancy assumption must reflect market reality — even in strong markets, allow 5–10%.
- Distinguish structural vacancy (always some void) from cyclical vacancy (demand-driven).
- Rent-free periods reduce effective rent below face rent; model them explicitly as zero-income months.
- Tenant improvements (TIs) are a significant hidden cost, especially for office and retail.

---

### 中文解释

**Pro Forma 结构是 Lecture 2 的核心框架，必须能流畅默写。**

**最容易失分的点：**
- OE（运营支出）中**不包括**折旧（Depreciation）和所得税（Income Tax）——这是会计概念，不是现金流出。
- CI（资本改善支出）容易被忽略——租户装修补贴（Tenant Improvements）和中介招租佣金（Leasing Commissions）是真实的大额现金支出。
- 空置率不能假设为零，即使是景气市场也要保留 5–10%。

**关键术语：** PGI 潜在总收入 / Vacancy Allowance 空置损失预留 / EGI（Effective Gross Income）有效总收入 / NOI 净营业收入 / Tenant Improvements (TIs) 租户装修补贴 / Leasing Commissions 招租佣金 / Rent-Free Period 免租期 / Face Rent vs Effective Rent 合同面租金 vs 有效租金

## Q1e — How to determine the re-sale value (10 pts)

*(Lecture 2)*

### Standard Answer

The most common method is the **Going-Out Cap Rate approach:**

$$V_{sale} = \frac{NOI_{n+1}}{\text{Going-Out Cap Rate}}$$

where NOI_{n+1} is the projected income in the year after the holding period ends, and the Going-Out Cap Rate is the market cap rate expected at exit.

**Key principles for the Going-Out Cap Rate:**
- It is typically **higher** than the Going-In Cap Rate, reflecting the building's age at exit and increased uncertainty about future cash flows.
- A common conservative assumption is Going-Out Cap Rate = Going-In Cap Rate + 25–50 bps.
- If significant capex improvements are made during the hold, Going-Out may approach Going-In.

**Why this is the riskiest DCF input:**
A small change in the Going-Out Cap Rate has a large impact on exit value. For example, with NOI = £1m: a cap rate change from 7% to 8% reduces the exit value from £14.3m to £12.5m — a 12.5% drop. This makes sensitivity analysis on the Going-Out Cap Rate essential.

Alternative methods: comparable transactions, replacement cost, Gordon Model (V = NOI / (r − g) for stabilised assets).

---

### 中文解释

**终值估算是 DCF 中不确定性最高的部分，通常占总价值 30–70%。**

**核心公式：** $V_{sale} = NOI_{n+1} / \text{Going-Out Cap Rate}$

**关键记忆点：**
- Going-Out Cap Rate 通常 > Going-In Cap Rate（楼宇老化 + 未来不确定性更大）
- Cap Rate 哪怕变动 1%，终值变化可达 12%+ → 敏感性分析（Sensitivity Analysis）必不可少
- Gordon 模型（$V = NOI/(r-g)$）适用于稳定增长阶段

**关键术语：** Going-Out Cap Rate 退出资本化率 / Going-In Cap Rate 进入资本化率 / Terminal Value 终值 / Sensitivity Analysis 敏感性分析 / Gordon Model 戈登模型

## Q1f — Sustainability/ESG: effect on NOI estimation and sale value (15 pts)

*(Lecture 10: S Burak Kaplanoglu, "ESG and Real Estate")*

### Standard Answer

Green investment trends have created a **two-tier market** where ESG credentials directly affect every line of the Pro Forma and the exit valuation:

**Effect on NOI:**
- **PGI/rents:** green-certified buildings (BREEAM/LEED) command a measurable rent premium — evidence from the lecture: London offices 11.6%, North America 7.1%, Asia 9.9% (JLL). Non-compliant "brown" assets face tenant flight and downward rent pressure.
- **Vacancy:** ESG-compliant buildings have lower vacancy rates as institutional tenants prioritise sustainability ("flight to green"). Brown assets face longer void periods.
- **Operating costs:** energy-efficient buildings have lower utility bills; however, upfront capital expenditure for retrofitting must be included in CI.

**Effect on sale value:**
- **Exit cap rate:** green assets attract more institutional buyers and trade at lower cap rates (higher prices). Brown assets face a wider cap rate and potential stranded asset risk — the lecture cites estimates of 3.4–10% housing value at risk from climate change.
- **Financing:** 73% of lenders have an ESG lending strategy; non-compliant assets face higher borrowing costs and restricted access to institutional capital, directly reducing leveraged returns.

**Overall:** ESG is no longer a soft consideration — it materially affects rents, vacancy, capex requirements, cap rates, and financing costs. A DCF that ignores ESG misprices the asset.

---

### 中文解释

**ESG 已经从"软指标"变成"硬财务因素"，直接影响 Pro Forma 的每一行和终值。**

**Pro Forma 层面影响：**
- PGI：绿色溢价（伦敦 11.6%，北美 7.1%，亚洲 9.9%）vs 棕色折价
- 空置：绿色资产租户更稳定；棕色资产空置期更长
- CI：节能改造资本支出需要显式建模

**终值层面影响：**
- 绿色资产：更低 Cap Rate → 更高终值
- 棕色资产：更高 Cap Rate + 搁浅资产风险（Stranded Asset Risk）

**关键术语：** Green Premium 绿色溢价 / Brown Discount 棕色折价 / Stranded Asset 搁浅资产 / ESG 环境-社会-治理 / Retrofitting CapEx 节能改造资本支出 / Flight to Green 租户绿色偏好迁移

## Q1 For Current Students — a: Why is development riskier than buying? (10 pts)

*(Lecture 4)*

### Standard Answer

Development carries more risk than buying an existing income-producing property because it introduces three categories of risk absent from stabilised acquisitions:

**1. Planning/permitting risk:** even after purchasing land, planning approval is not guaranteed. The process takes 6 months to 2+ years and can be rejected entirely, causing total loss of sunk costs.

**2. Construction cost and time overruns:** hard costs (materials, labour) are difficult to predict precisely — material price swings, unforeseen site conditions, and contractor claims drive overspend. Construction loans accrue interest daily via negative amortisation; every month of delay adds rolled-up interest to the total cost.

**3. Lease-up risk:** at completion, market conditions may have deteriorated. The developer may have built exactly what the market no longer wants — a "build it and they won't come" outcome. This risk cannot be hedged at the time the project begins.

Additionally, development has a uniquely adverse cash flow profile: large outflows during construction with zero income, followed eventually by positive cash flows — the opposite of a stabilised asset.

---

### 中文解释

**开发三大额外风险（买楼没有，开发才有）：**

1. 规划审批风险（Planning Risk）：买了地也未必能建，审批可能耗时数年或直接被拒。
2. 建设成本/工期超支（Cost & Time Overrun）：建设贷款每日负摊销（Negative Amortisation），工期每延误一天都在增加总成本。
3. 完工出租风险（Lease-Up Risk）：竣工时市场可能已转向，建好的东西未必租得出去。

**现金流特征：** 先大量流出（建设期），后才流入（出租稳定后）——与买楼即收租相反。

**关键术语：** Planning Risk 规划审批风险 / Cost Overrun 成本超支 / Negative Amortisation 负摊销（利息滚入本金）/ Lease-Up Risk 出租风险 / Hard Costs 硬成本（材料人工）/ Soft Costs 软成本（专业费用）

## Q1 For Current Students — b: Interim and permanent finance in development (15 pts)

*(Lecture 4)*

### Standard Answer

Development projects require two distinct types of financing that serve different phases:

**Interim (construction) finance:**
- Provided by commercial banks with development lending capabilities.
- Disbursed progressively as construction milestones are met — the bank draws down funds in tranches, verified by independent surveyors, ensuring the loan balance never exceeds the value of completed work.
- During construction, the borrower typically cannot make interest payments (no income). Interest is therefore rolled into the principal — **negative amortisation**. The loan balance grows throughout construction.
- Higher interest rate than permanent finance, reflecting construction-phase risk (no tangible collateral yet, completion uncertainty).

**Permanent (take-out) finance:**
- Provided by long-term investors such as insurance companies and pension funds, who need long-duration assets to match their long-duration liabilities.
- Replaces the construction loan upon completion, when the property is income-producing.
- Crucially, the permanent loan commitment must be secured **before construction begins** — the construction lender will not advance funds unless confident the construction loan will be repaid at completion.
- Conditional on meeting thresholds: completion by agreed date, minimum occupancy (e.g., 80%+ leased), minimum rent levels. If conditions are not met, the permanent lender can walk away.

**Key interdependency:** the inability to secure a permanent loan commitment effectively blocks the entire project, even if the construction loan is available.

---

### 中文解释

**开发融资 = 两阶段：建设贷款（过渡）→ 永久贷款（长期）**

| | 建设贷款 | 永久贷款 |
|--|---------|---------|
| 提供方 | 商业银行 | 保险公司、养老金 |
| 期限 | 短期（12–36月） | 长期（5–25年）|
| 利率 | 高（风险溢价） | 低（稳定资产）|
| 放款方式 | 分批按工程进度 | 竣工达标后一次性 |
| 本金还款 | 负摊销（利息滚入本金）| 正常摊还 |

**最重要的逻辑：** 必须在开工前拿到永久贷款承诺（Take-Out Commitment）——因为建设贷款方只有在确信"有人接盘"的情况下才愿意放款。

**关键术语：** Negative Amortisation 负摊销 / Progressive Drawdown 分批放款 / Take-Out Commitment 永久贷款承诺 / Balloon Payment 气球还款（到期一次性还本）

## Q1 For Current Students — c: "Build at 10, sell at 8" rule (15 pts)

*(Lecture 4)*

### Standard Answer

This rule describes the return threshold that makes development viable and profitable:

**"Build at 10" — Going-In Cap Rate on cost:**
$$\text{Going-In Cap Rate} = \frac{\text{Stabilised NOI}}{\text{Total Development Cost}} \geq 10\%$$
Development only makes sense if the stabilised NOI divided by total cost (land + hard costs + soft costs) yields at least 10%. This 10% compensates for all development risks — planning, construction, and lease-up uncertainty.

**"Sell at 8" — Going-Out Cap Rate on exit:**
$$\text{Exit Value} = \frac{\text{Stabilised NOI}}{8\%}$$
Once built and stabilised, the asset is less risky (uncertainty resolved), so investors accept a lower return — they price it at a lower cap rate (8%), paying more per £1 of income.

**The development profit:**
$$\text{Profit} = \frac{NOI}{8\%} - \frac{NOI}{10\%} = NOI \times \left(\frac{1}{0.08} - \frac{1}{0.10}\right)$$
With NOI = £1m: Exit value = £12.5m − Cost = £10m → **Profit = £2.5m (25% return on cost)**

The 2% cap rate spread is the developer's compensation for bearing all development risks. If market cap rates compress (e.g., market buys at 6%), the spread and profit widen. If cap rates rise, the spread narrows — potentially making development unviable.

---

### 中文解释

**"10建8卖"法则是判断开发可行性的核心工具。**

**逻辑核心：**
- 建设阶段风险高（不确定性大）→ 要求 10% 回报
- 建成稳定后风险低（租约已签）→ 买家接受 8% 回报
- 这 2% 的 Cap Rate 差创造开发利润

**数值示例（必须记住）：**
NOI = £1m → 建设成本 = £10m（at 10%）→ 出售价值 = £12.5m（at 8%）→ 利润 = £2.5m（25%）

**实用意义：** 用 Going-In Cap Rate 快速判断当前租金/成本条件下项目是否可行，而无需完整 DCF。

**关键术语：** Going-In Cap Rate 进入资本化率（基于成本）/ Going-Out Cap Rate 退出资本化率（基于市价）/ Development Spread 开发利差 / Development Profit 开发利润

# QUESTION 2 — Mortgage Calculations

**Background:** Property £1,000,000. Own funds £750,000 → Loan = **£250,000**. Annual income £57,500 → Monthly income = **£4,791.67**. 20-year term, monthly compounding (N = 240). DCR ≥ 1.5; Income Multiple ≤ 4.5.

---

## Q2a — IO loan: monthly payment and qualification (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

**IO monthly payment** (rate = 12% p.a., i = 1%/month):
$$PMT_{IO} = £250{,}000 \times 1\% = \mathbf{£2{,}500/month}$$
Last payment = £2,500 + £250,000 (principal repaid at maturity) = **£252,500**

**Qualification:**
- Income Multiple: Max loan = 4.5 × £57,500 = £258,750 ≥ £250,000 ✓
- DCR = £4,791.67 / £2,500 = **1.92 ≥ 1.5** ✓

**The borrower qualifies for the IO loan.**

---

### 中文解释

IO（Interest Only）纯息贷款：每月只还利息，到期一次性还清本金£250,000。月供 = 本金 × 月利率 = £250,000 × 1% = £2,500。

**关键术语：** IO（Interest Only）纯息贷款 / Balloon Payment 气球还款（到期还本）/ DCR（Debt Coverage Ratio）偿债覆盖率 / Income Multiple 收入倍数

---

## Q2b — CPM loan: monthly payment and qualification (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

**CPM monthly payment** (rate = 10% p.a., i = 10%/12, N = 240):
$$PMT_{CPM} = 250{,}000 \times \frac{0.008333}{1-(1.008333)^{-240}} = \mathbf{£2{,}412.55/month}$$

**Qualification:**
- Income Multiple: £250,000 ≤ £258,750 ✓
- DCR = £4,791.67 / £2,412.55 = **1.99 ≥ 1.5** ✓

**The borrower qualifies. Note CPM monthly payment (£2,412.55) is lower than IO (£2,500)** despite the loan being the same — because CPM amortises principal, while IO only pays interest at a higher rate.

---

### 中文解释

**CPM（Constant Payment Mortgage）等额还款：** 每月固定还款，逐渐增加本金摊还比例。月供 £2,412.55 < IO月供 £2,500——因为 CPM 利率更低（10% vs 12%）。

**关键术语：** CPM（Constant Payment Mortgage）等额还款 / Amortisation 本金摊还

---

## Q2c — Why lower rate on CPM than IO? (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

The bank charges a lower rate on CPM (10% vs 12%) because **CPM is less risky for the lender:**

**1. Amortisation reduces outstanding balance over time.** Under IO, the bank's exposure remains £250,000 for 20 years. Under CPM, each payment includes principal repayment — the outstanding balance falls monthly, reducing the bank's risk progressively.

**2. Shorter effective duration.** CPM returns principal steadily, so the bank's average time-weighted exposure is much shorter than IO. Less duration = less interest rate risk.

**3. No balloon payment risk.** IO requires the borrower to repay £250,000 in full at maturity. If the property value has fallen or the borrower cannot refinance, the bank faces full principal loss. CPM has zero balance at maturity.

**Borrower preferences:**
- IO: lower early monthly payments, higher flexibility, good for investors expecting capital appreciation.
- CPM: forced equity accumulation, no balloon risk, lower total interest cost — preferred for owner-occupiers with stable income.

---

### 中文解释

**利率差异 = 风险差异：** IO 整个期间银行敞口不变（£250,000），CPM 每月下降 → 银行风险逐渐降低 → 接受更低利率。

**三点核心：** ① 摊销降低敞口 ② 有效久期更短 ③ 无气球还款风险。

**关键术语：** Duration 久期 / Balloon Payment Risk 气球还款风险 / Equity Accumulation 本金积累 / Risk Premium 风险溢价

---

## Q2d — ECB with 1% discount point, held to maturity (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

**Net proceeds** = £250,000 × 99% = **£247,500** (1% origination charge deducted upfront).

**IO held to maturity:**
CFs: −£247,500 at t=0; £2,500 × 239 months; £252,500 at month 240.
→ **ECB ≈ 12.13%**

**CPM held to maturity:**
CFs: −£247,500 at t=0; £2,412.55 × 240 months.
→ **ECB ≈ 10.14%**

| Loan | Stated Rate | ECB (maturity) | Increase |
|------|------------|----------------|---------|
| IO | 12% | 12.13% | +13 bps |
| CPM | 10% | 10.14% | +14 bps |

ECB exceeds the stated rate because the upfront fee reduces net proceeds while payments are unchanged — the lender effectively earns more than the stated rate.

---

### 中文解释

**ECB = 借款人实际现金流的 IRR。** 起始费用（1% = £2,500）使实际到手只有 £247,500，但月供按 £250,000 计算 → ECB > 名义利率。

**工具：** TI BA II+ 的 CF Worksheet。输入 CF0 = −247,500，后续各期现金流，CPT IRR × 12 = ECB。

**关键术语：** ECB（Effective Cost of Borrowing）有效借款成本 / Origination Charge 起始费用（折扣点）/ Net Proceeds 实际到手金额

## Q2e — ECB at 10 years and 5 years (including 2% prepayment penalty) (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

Prepayment penalty = 2% of outstanding balance.

**IO loans:** balance always = £250,000; penalty = £5,000.

| | Repay at 10yr | Repay at 5yr |
|-|--------------|-------------|
| IO ECB | **≈ 12.28%** | **≈ 12.56%** |
| CPM ECB | **≈ 10.27%** | **≈ 10.56%** |

*(CPM outstanding balances: B₁₂₀ ≈ £182,561; B₆₀ ≈ £224,506. Add 2% penalty before entering as terminal CF.)*

**Pattern: earlier repayment → higher ECB** for both loan types. The fixed origination fee and penalty are amortised over fewer periods, increasing the annualised cost.

---

### 中文解释

**规律：提前还款越早，ECB 越高。** 起始费用（固定成本）+ 提前还款违约金（2%）分摊到更少的月份 → 年化成本更高。

**CPM 提前还款需先用 AMORT Worksheet 求余额，再乘以 1.02（含违约金）作为期末现金流。**

**关键术语：** Prepayment Penalty 提前还款违约金 / Outstanding Balance 剩余本金 / Fixed Cost Front-Loading 固定成本前置效应

## Q2f — Why do banks impose origination charges? (5 pts)

### Standard Answer

Banks charge origination fees for three reasons:

1. **Prevent adverse prepayment:** when interest rates fall, borrowers want to refinance at lower rates — the bank receives early principal repayment just when reinvestment rates are worst. The origination fee raises the effective cost of early repayment, discouraging refinancing precisely when it hurts the bank most.

2. **Screen for less mobile borrowers:** borrowers willing to pay an upfront fee signal they intend to hold the loan long-term. This filters out opportunistic refinancers and gives the bank a more stable loan book.

3. **Recover fixed origination costs:** every loan involves fixed upfront costs (credit checks, documentation, systems). The fee ensures these are recovered even if the loan is repaid early.

---

### 中文解释

**银行收取起始费用的核心目的：防止利率下降时借款人集中再融资（Refinancing）。** 提前还款越早 ECB 越高（Q2e 的规律）正是这一抑制机制的体现。

**关键术语：** Adverse Prepayment 不利的提前还款 / Refinancing 再融资 / Screening 筛选 / Less Mobile Borrowers 不频繁再融资的稳定借款人

## Q2g — Capped CPM at £1,900/month: FV at maturity and ECB (15 pts)

### Standard Answer

Normal CPM month 1 interest = £250,000 × (10%/12) = **£2,083.33**

Since the cap (£1,900) **< first month's interest** (£2,083.33), the payment does not cover interest — the shortfall (£183.33) is added to principal. This is **negative amortisation**: the balance grows rather than shrinks.

**FV at maturity** (using TVM: PV = −£250,000, PMT = £1,900, N = 240, I = 10%):
$$\boxed{FV \approx £389{,}217.62}$$

The balance has grown from £250,000 to £389,218 — 56% increase due to compounding negative amortisation.

**ECB** (net proceeds £247,500; 239 payments of £1,900; final payment £1,900 + £389,217.62):
$$\boxed{ECB \approx 10.10\%}$$

Slightly lower than standard CPM (10.14%) because early cash flows are lower — they are discounted more heavily in the IRR calculation. However, the total amount repaid is far greater.

---

### 中文解释

**负摊销（Negative Amortisation）核心机制：** 月供 £1,900 < 第一月利息 £2,083 → 缺口 £183 滚入本金 → 本金不降反升 → 到期余额 £389,218（远超初始 £250,000）。

**注意：** ECB（10.10%）略低于普通 CPM（10.14%），因为早期月供更低，折现权重大的早期现金流更小。但总还款额远更多——这是"今天少付、未来多还"的权衡。

**关键术语：** Negative Amortisation 负摊销 / Payment Cap 月供上限 / Front-Loading 前置效应

## Q2h — Capped CPM: ECB at 10 years and 5 years (10 pts)

### Standard Answer

For a capped CPM, the AMORT worksheet cannot be used (PMT ≠ standard CPM payment). Use TVM to compute FV at the repayment date instead.

**5-year balance** (N=60, I=10%, PV=−250,000, PMT=1,900 → FV): **≈ £264,197**
With 2% prepayment penalty: £264,197 × 1.02 = **£269,481**
→ **ECB ≈ 10.57%**

**10-year balance** (N=120): **≈ £287,555**
With 2% penalty: £293,306
→ **ECB ≈ 10.28%**

| | Standard CPM | Capped CPM |
|-|-------------|-----------|
| Maturity | 10.14% | 10.10% |
| 10 years | 10.27% | 10.28% |
| 5 years | 10.56% | 10.57% |

Pattern is the same: earlier repayment → higher ECB. Capped CPM closely tracks standard CPM because the same rate applies; the negative amortisation effect is small in percentage terms.

**关键区别：** 上限 CPM 不能用 AMORT Worksheet（因为 PMT 已被固定为非标准值），必须用 TVM 的 FV 功能计算指定时点余额。

规律与普通 CPM 一致：提前还款 → ECB 升高。

**关键术语：** TVM（Time Value of Money）时间价值 / FV（Future Value）未来值 / Prepayment Penalty 提前还款违约金

## Q2i — ARM (8%/10%/12%): payments and ECB (15 pts)

*(Lecture 7)*

### Standard Answer

ARM = **partitioned CPM**: at each rate reset, recalculate PMT using remaining balance and remaining term.

**Stage 1: Months 1–24, rate = 8%, N = 240**
$$PMT_1 = £2{,}091.00 \qquad B_{24} = £238{,}993$$

**Stage 2: Months 25–48, rate = 10%, N = 216**
$$PMT_2 = £2{,}389.56 \qquad B_{48} = £228{,}468.50$$

**Stage 3: Months 49–240, rate = 12%, N = 192**
$$PMT_3 = £2{,}681.59$$

**ECB** (CF Worksheet: CF0 = −247,500; C01=2,091×24; C02=2,389.56×24; C03=2,681.59×192):
$$\boxed{ECB \approx 10.57\%}$$

The ECB falls between the three stage rates (8%, 10%, 12%), weighted by period length and time-discounting — early periods carry more PV weight, pulling the blended rate toward the lower early-stage rates.

---

### 中文解释

**ARM = 分段 CPM。** 每次利率重置时，以当时剩余本金和剩余期限重新计算月供。三步走：① 第一期月供+期末余额 → ② 以余额和剩余期限算第二期 → ③ 同理第三期。

**关键术语：** ARM（Adjustable Rate Mortgage）可调利率抵押贷款 / Partitioned CPM 分段等额还款 / Rate Reset 利率重置

## Q2j — ARM with £1,900 payment cap in first two years (15 pts)

*(Lecture 7)*

### Standard Answer

With £1,900 cap in months 1–24 at 8%, the payment is below the interest due (£2,091 normal payment). This creates negative amortisation in Stage 1 — the balance after month 24 is higher than in Q2i.

**Stage 1 balance** (TVM: N=24, I=8%, PV=−250,000, PMT=1,900 → FV): **≈ £243,949**
(vs £238,993 without cap — £4,956 higher due to negative amortisation)

**Stage 2: rate = 10%, N = 216, PV = 243,949**
$$PMT_2 \approx £2{,}439.11 \qquad B_{48} \approx £233{,}206$$

**Stage 3: rate = 12%, N = 192, PV = 233,206**
$$PMT_3 \approx £2{,}737.20$$

**ECB** (CF Worksheet: CF0 = −247,500; C01=1,900×24; C02=2,439.11×24; C03=2,737.20×192):
$$\boxed{ECB \approx 10.69\%}$$

**Higher than Q2i (10.57%)** because the Stage 1 negative amortisation increases the principal, which then accrues interest at the higher Stage 2 and 3 rates (10%, 12%). The saving from lower early payments is more than offset by higher future payments on the larger outstanding balance.

---

### 中文解释

**第一阶段月供上限 £1,900 < 正常月供 £2,091 → 负摊销 → 期末余额更高 → 以更高利率（10%/12%）计息 → 总成本更高（ECB 10.69% > Q2i 的 10.57%）。**

核心教训：月供上限的"今日节省"被后期更高本金的利息代价所抵消。

**关键术语：** Payment Cap 月供上限 / Negative Amortisation 负摊销 / Compounding Effect 复利效应
